## Concepto Aether

`dist/assets/aether-concept.png` es una imagen generada con la herramienta integrada de imágenes el 28 de septiembre de 2026. Ilustra una demostración ficticia; no es una captura de un videojuego existente.

Prompt: “Create a premium cinematic game environment concept art for a fictional exploration game called Aether. Wide horizontal 16:9 composition. An immense dark alien valley, obsidian basalt rocks and floating monolithic stone arches, a tiny lone explorer with a coral orange cloak standing near the lower left, looking toward a spectacular luminous pale lavender circular celestial portal on the right horizon. Misty graphite purple mountains, sparse luminous lime-green vegetation along the path, dramatic soft volumetric light and deep shadows. Sophisticated art direction, photoreal Unreal Engine environment concept quality, rich tactile stone detail, quiet monumental scale. Palette predominantly dark desaturated charcoal and muted violet, pale lavender portal and restrained coral / lime accents. No text, no lettering, no logo, no UI, no watermarks. This is a portfolio demonstration visual, not an existing game. The composition must have a striking readable silhouette at thumbnail size.”

## Gráficos realizados con código

La escultura de portada está dibujada en Canvas. Las interfaces de Nexo y Orbit están realizadas en HTML/CSS y contienen datos de muestra. Los tres colores y las tres barras del emblema aluden a los tres fundadores.
