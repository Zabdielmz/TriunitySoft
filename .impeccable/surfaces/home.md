# Triunity / Home

Mode: Persuade with a project gallery. Implementation: React, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS and Motion.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Three founders and three disciplines converge into one team that builds software, web experiences and games.

OWN-WORLD: Near-black developer studio, self-hosted modern sans plus mono, restrained cyan/violet/magenta signals, crisp code windows and a luminous geometric core. User-provided black wordmark and “Jugamos en serio. Creamos con pasión.” establish the brand reference.

STORY: Understand the offer in seconds, learn the trident, scan three disciplines, explore clearly labeled demonstration concepts, see the delivery process, and start a conversation.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Clear headline and two actions on a protected dark left zone. The refined supplied banner fills the entire hero as a soft atmospheric image; its lettering blends into the right side without a visible image boundary. On narrow screens, cyan and violet light from that image fills the upper area while the copy remains on a dark field, with a restrained sweep across the light. Reduced-motion settings keep it still.

FORM: Code-led build. Signature interaction: integrated brand atmosphere and a two-row concept portfolio that pauses for inspection. On touch and with reduced motion the cards scroll manually.

FINISH: Verify 360, 768, 1280 and 1920 px; run lint and build; document all placeholders and generated asset provenance.
